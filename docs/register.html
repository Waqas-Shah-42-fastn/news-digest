Get the daily digest in Slack

News Digest is a once-a-day roundup of what matters, built on fastn and posted to a Slack channel of your choice. Every edition is also published on this site.

What's in it

  • 🔝 Top stories: the headlines covered by the most outlets.
  • 🌍 Geopolitics: world affairs, diplomacy and conflict.
  • 💼 Business: markets, companies and the economy.
  • 💻 Technology: products, platforms and security.
  • 🤖 AI companies: news about the labs and companies building AI.
  • 👽 Reddit: the top posts of the day.
  • 🛠️ Engineering blogs: new posts from teams such as Netflix, Stripe, Cloudflare and GitHub.

How registration works

  1. Request an invite Click the button below. It opens a short issue form on GitHub, so you need a GitHub account.
  2. Get your setup link Within 6 hours a bot comments a personal, single-use fastn setup link on your issue.
  3. Connect Slack Open the link, connect your Slack workspace, pick a channel and, if you like, adjust which sections and sources you get.
  4. Read it every day The digest arrives in your channel daily at the time you chose.
Request an invite

Opens a GitHub issue form. Issues in this repository are public, so only include what you are happy to share.

Your setup link is single-use and expires after 2 hours (issues are public, so links are kept short-lived). If it expires before you use it, comment new link on your issue and the bot will send a fresh one.
